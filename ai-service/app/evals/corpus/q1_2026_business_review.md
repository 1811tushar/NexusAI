# Q1 2026 Business Review

## Summary
Total revenue for Q1 2026 (January to March) was $2.77 million ($2,768,725). Trading was stable and in line with plan across all four regions.

## Revenue by region
| Region | Q1 2026 revenue |
|---|---|
| North | $729,980 |
| South | $655,495 |
| East | $582,125 |
| West | $801,125 |

## Categories
Home, Electronics and Apparel all met their Q1 targets. West Home revenue was $345,150, the strongest Home result of any region.

## Marketing
Marketing spend in Q1 followed the H1 plan. South marketing spend was $68,364 for the quarter.
